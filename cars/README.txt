Put car pictures in this folder and they appear automatically.

How the file name is made: the make, a dash, then the model, all lowercase, with dashes
instead of spaces, ending in .jpg. The app tries the full model first, then just the
first word of the model.

Examples
  Toyota Fortuner        ->  toyota-fortuner.jpg
  Suzuki Cultus AGS      ->  suzuki-cultus-ags.jpg
  Honda Civic RS         ->  honda-civic-rs.jpg
  Kia Sportage Alpha     ->  kia-sportage-alpha.jpg   (or kia-sportage.jpg)
  Toyota Corolla Altis   ->  toyota-corolla-altis.jpg (or toyota-corolla.jpg)
  Hyundai Tucson         ->  hyundai-tucson.jpg
  Suzuki Alto VXL        ->  suzuki-alto-vxl.jpg

A picture uploaded to one specific car (in the car's Photos section) always wins over
the picture here. A car with neither shows the plain coloured card.

Only use pictures you have the right to use: your own photos, your supplier's pictures
with permission, or licensed images. Keep each file under about 300 KB (about 1000 pixels
wide) so the pages stay fast.
